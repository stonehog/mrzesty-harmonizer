Edits made for MrZesty: leading silence removed, trimmed to a short one-shot length, short fade-out, converted to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV, normalized to −1 dBFS peak. Pitched zones (bass, piano, vibes) were measured for their root note. Electric guitar roots follow the FreePats SFZ `pitch_keycenter` values.
