Warnings & notes

    Please read: every pressure number here is an estimate worked out from speed — not a measurement. This tool does not give load recipes. Always follow published load data from the powder and bullet makers, start low, work up slowly, and stop at the first sign of pressure (hard bolt lift, flattened primers, ejector marks).
